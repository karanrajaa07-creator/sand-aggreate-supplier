Commercial Terms of Supply & Haulage

Standard terms governing mineral and aggregate supply agreements, site access clearance, offload demurrage, and weighbridge calibration.

1. Quotations and Order Confirmation

Written quotations issued by Vanguard Aggregates & Minerals are valid for thirty (30) days from issuance. Binding contracts are formed upon written order confirmation and acceptance of site access conditions. Prices quoted are exclusive of VAT.

2. Delivery Site Access and Offloading

The purchaser is responsible for ensuring clear, safe, hardstanding vehicular access to the designated tipping or crane placement point:

  • Width & Clearance: Minimum 3.2m entrance width for 8-wheel rigid tippers and 4.0m for articulated bulk vehicles.
  • Overhead Hazards: High-voltage cables, tree canopies, and architectural lintels must clear 7.8m vertical tipping envelopes.
  • Demurrage: A standard 30-minute offload window is included with each delivery. Additional waiting time is charged at standard demurrage rates.

3. Weight and Measurement Verification

All bulk loose materials are measured over certified, Weights & Measures approved quarry weighbridges. Weighbridge conveyance tickets are conclusive evidence of quantity delivered unless notified in writing within 24 hours of delivery.

4. Material Quality and Testing Standards

Materials conform to relevant British and European standards (BS EN 12620, BS EN 13242, Specification for Highway Works Clause 803/805). Factory Production Control (FPC) laboratory certificates are available on request.
