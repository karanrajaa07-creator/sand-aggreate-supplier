Safety & Access Policy

Crucial Site Access Checklist for Contractors

A fully loaded 8-wheel tipper weighs up to 32,000 kg (32 tonnes). To protect site personnel, domestic driveways, and subterranean utility services, please review these mandatory access requirements before booking.

Driver Authority Protocol: Our HGV drivers carry legal responsibility for vehicle and public safety. If a site access route presents an unmitigated risk of overturning, power cable strike, or sinking into uncompacted ground, the driver is empowered to abort or request an alternate safe discharge location.
01

Gateway & Access Road Clearances

Clear gateway aperture must be a minimum of 3.2 meters wide for 8-wheel tippers and grab lorries (3.0m for Hiab). Take into account protruding brick pillars, parked commuter vehicles on narrow lanes, and gate latch hinges.

02

Overhead Cables & Tree Limbs (Severe Hazard)

Tippers require an extreme vertical tipping clearance of up to 7.8 meters. Never request tipping beneath overhead high-voltage power lines, low telecom telephone wires, or mature overhanging oak trees.

03

Ground Stability & Subgrade Bearing

Wet mud, uncompacted soil, and gravel paths can cause 32-tonne vehicles to sink axle-deep. Driveways must be reinforced concrete, asphalt, or stabilized hardcore. For soft ground, lay steel road plates or heavy timber bog mats.

04

Manholes, Drain Covers & Septic Chambers

Domestic plastic drainage inspection chambers and shallow septic tanks will collapse under HGV wheel loadings. Point out all subterranean inspection chambers to the driver prior to vehicle entry.

05

Public Highway Drops & Council Permits

If aggregates or bulk bags must be placed on a public pavement or carriageway, the property owner is legally required to obtain a council highway permit and display safety warning cones and lamps.
